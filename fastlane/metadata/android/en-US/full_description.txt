<i>SecondsClock</i> provides customizable clock widgets for your home screen and a full-screen night clock.

<br><br><b>Features:</b>

<ul>
<li>Choose digital or analog clocks with seconds and an optional next alarm.</li>
<li>Use locale-aware layouts or create custom date and time formats.</li>
<li>Give each widget its own layout, colours, and actions.</li>
<li>Use low-light dimming and keep the full-screen clock on while charging.</li>
<li>Save and reuse settings with presets or JSON backup and restore.</li>
</ul>

No ads, in-app purchases, or donation prompts.
